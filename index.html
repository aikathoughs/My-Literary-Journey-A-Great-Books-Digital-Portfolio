Hello! I am Angelaica Mae P. Harochoc, a fourth-year Information Technology student at the University of Nueva Caceres. I study how to build systems and software, but I also love stories and art. In my free time, I make digital and traditional art. I like graphic design, and I enjoy drawing in a realistic style. Great Books gave me a chance to read old stories from different parts of the world and see how they still speak to us today.

I love ancient and old stories, especially those that teach deep lessons or hide meaning like a riddle. I enjoy thinking about what a story is really trying to say. My love for art also changes how I read. When I read a scene, I like to picture it like a painting, with its colors, faces, and feelings. Before this course, I mostly read stories for fun. Now I also read them to understand people, history, and society.

When I started Great Books, I expected to have fun and to learn about literature I did not know yet. I wanted to meet new characters, new cultures, and new ways of thinking. I also hoped to become a better reader who can look deeper than the plot.

— Angelaica
